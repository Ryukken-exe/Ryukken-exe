## 五輪 — Five Principles

> **Observe carefully.**  
> Understand the problem before writing the solution.

> **Cut only once.**  
> Every line should have purpose.

> **Remain adaptable.**  
> Strong architecture bends without breaking.

> **Discipline over speed.**  
> Consistency outlasts intensity.

> **Master the fundamentals.**  
> Simplicity is the highest form of efficiency.
